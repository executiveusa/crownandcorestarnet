# Crown & Core Brand

## One job
Provide the canonical, protected brand inputs used by the website and other public-facing work.

## Inputs
- approved owner-supplied brand assets;
- verified Crown & Core public materials;
- approved brand decisions.

## Process
1. Prefer original approved assets over recreated versions.
2. Preserve source files unchanged.
3. Record provenance and usage notes.
4. Separate canonical originals from generated derivatives.

## Outputs
- `assets/` — canonical originals and provenance;
- future brand decisions only when approved.

## Human check
Any logo redesign, recolor, distortion, replacement, or new identity direction requires explicit approval.

## Do NOT load
Runtime internals, unrelated district outputs, or speculative brand concepts.
