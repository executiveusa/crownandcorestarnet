# _shared

## Job

Hold Crown & Core truth and references that multiple districts may read.

## Rules

- One home per fact.
- Evidence and fact are separate.
- Unknown remains unknown.
- Public evidence is not authenticated business data.
- Health claims require appropriate professional review before promotion.

## Intended homes

- `client/` — verified business facts, model, journey
- `evidence/` — sourced observations
- `policy/` — Crown & Core-specific execution rules
- `references/` — shared research/method references

## Human check

Owner/business facts must be verified before being promoted from evidence or assumption into client truth.
