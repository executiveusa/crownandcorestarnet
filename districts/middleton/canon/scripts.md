# Middleton scripts and patterns

These are source-derived patterns. They are **not automatically approved Crown & Core copy**.

## Reactivation pattern

A direct, specific reactivation offer with a real deadline and a clear booking path.

Crown & Core adaptation must use real service eligibility, real pricing/offer terms, owner approval, and valid consent.

## Discovery questions

- Do you have a CRM or booking system that tracks leads and customers?
- How many website visitors and inquiries did you receive in the last 30 days?
- What is an average customer worth by service?
- What was revenue in the last 30 days?
- How are reviews requested today?
- How are referrals requested today?
- What happens after a website inquiry?
- What happens after a missed call?
- What happens after a no-show?
- What happens after a customer has not returned for 90+ days?

## Gap recap pattern

Restate the verified gap in the owner's own numbers, explain its likely business cost, then propose one bounded test.

## Core sales frame

Optimize the digital storefront, trust signals, and follow-up so more interested people actually reach the door and become customers.

## Source handling

Exact source quotes and video IDs are preserved in `system.json`. Before using any quote publicly, verify the source and context.
