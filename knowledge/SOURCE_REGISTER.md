# Crown & Core — source register and reconciliation rules

## Source precedence

1. **Current authenticated, owner-authorized business records** with a verifiable retrieval receipt, legal permission and tenant scope. Not yet connected here.
2. **Explicit current owner/clinical/legal approvals** bound to exact statements, scope and revision. Not present as executable approvals in this repo.
3. **Dated public evidence snapshots**, including `_shared/client/PUBLIC_TRUTH.md` and `_shared/evidence/`. Historical; not necessarily current.
4. **Internal operating plans** such as `_system/references/PRD.md`, `districts/` and the Crown & Core growth-system issue. These express goals, not completed results.
5. **Proposals/working drafts**, including the 90-day agreement and creative concepts. They are not proof of client signature or program activation.

Where sources conflict, preserve both, date each and mark `REQUIRES_OWNER_RECONCILIATION`. Never silently pick the more convenient claim.
